# SURVIVE TO EXIT - Platformer X Space 

**Survive To Exit** is a 2D space platformer and rocket-landing game built in Unity. The game combines a troll-style platformer inspired by Level Devil with a precision rocket-landing simulator. Every stage is designed to be frustrating, huge, difficult, and fun—built to test patience and temper across 20 intense levels

---

## How the Game Works : 

Each Levels has Two Main Parts : 
1. **Astronaut Platforming:** Navigating tilemap stages on foot, jumping across gaps, collecting coins and gems, avoiding hidden traps, and reaching the rocket.
2. **Rocket Flying:** Piloting the rocket through tight spaces, managing fuel, dodging hazards, and landing safely on the landing pad without crashing or flying too fast.

---

## FEATURES : 

* **20 HandCrafted levels:** Starts manageable and ramps up into massive, frustrating stages. More awesome levels are planned for future updates.
* **Lasers & Deadly Obstacles:** 
* **Dual Timer System:**  Flashing laser balls,laser gates, rotating traps, and instant-death zones, moving traps.
* **Teleportation Portals:** Portals for player side - that warp the player from one side to another.
* **Jumping Spring Levels:** Bouncy spring that lauch the player high into the air.
 * **Level Countdown Timer:** Every level has a strict time limit. Running out of time triggers a Time Out screen.
  * **Total Game Timer:** A global speedrun clock tracks total time from Level 1 all the way to the Level 20 completion screen.
* **5-Star Rating System:** Finishing a level awards 1 to 5 stars based on how fast the stage was cleared compared to its target time.
* **Fuel, Coins & Gems:** Limited rocket fuel requires careful thrusting, while coins and gems are scattered across risky spots.
* **Music & Sound Effects:** Separate background tracks for menus, platforming, and rocket flight, along with sound effects for explosions, thrusters, lasers, pickups, and UI clicks.  Etc.... 

---

## CONTROLS : 

DON'T RELAY ON THESE KEYS, BECAUSE THERE ARE CONTROLS LEVELS , WHERE THE CONTORLS CAN BE OPPOSITE AND CHANGE... 

- **A** / **Left Arrow** - Move left
  - **D** / **Right Arrow** - Move Right
  - **SpaceBar / Up Arrow / W Key** - Jump
  - **SpaceBar 2 Times** - Double Jump - In Some Levels
  - **T Key** - Pause / Resume.
  - **E Key Hold** - For some Levels to unlock Red Doors..
